What the business does

A large asset, divided into digital tokens

MOOZARS is building regulated infrastructure to tokenise real-world assets — starting with energy and commodities, and extending to property and other assets. Each token is designed to be backed by a share of the real asset, settled using a regulated stablecoin.

Tokens backed by the real asset

Each digital token is designed to be backed by a portion of a real commodity, held in safekeeping behind it.

Paid in seconds, not days

Payment uses a stablecoin — a digital form of money that always holds the same value, fully backed by cash. Settlement is designed to take seconds rather than the 2–5 days of traditional banking, so capital is freed at once.

Energy first, then more

The first market is energy, because oil is already traded worldwide every day. The same method then extends to other commodities, property and further assets.

How it works

One cargo, from funding to repayment

Today, a crude oil cargo worth well over US$100 million can be financed only by a few of the world’s largest trading firms and banks. MOOZARS is designed to open that financing to many participants together. Here is the intended journey of a single cargo.

1

Divided into tokens

The cargo is divided into digital tokens, each backed by a portion of the oil.

2

Funded and paid

The cargo is funded by many together. The seller is paid at once, with a permanent record of every transaction.

3

Proceeds returned

Proceeds return to token holders when the cargo settles. The same method is intended to extend to other real-world assets.

Liquidity

Built on an asset that is already liquid

Tokenising an asset makes it easier to divide and move, but it cannot create a market on its own — tokens are only worth something if there are real buyers for what sits beneath them.

Energy is one of the deepest, most actively traded markets in the world. The buyers and sellers already exist.
Every day

Crude oil trades continuously across the world, among thousands of buyers and sellers.

Already there

MOOZARS does not need to create demand for the asset — it connects to a market that already exists.

Why Hong Kong, and why now

Why Hong Kong

MOOZARS intends to seek a stablecoin licence from the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA), Hong Kong’s central bank and financial regulator. Hong Kong has a dedicated stablecoin law and sits at the centre of Asian trade and energy. No licence is held and no application has yet been submitted.
