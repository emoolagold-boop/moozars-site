Real-World Asset Settlement Infrastructure

MOOZARS is building regulated infrastructure to tokenise real-world assets — starting with energy and commodities, and extending to property and other assets. Each token is designed to be backed by a share of the real asset, settled using a regulated stablecoin. Platform is in development; no operational service is currently offered.

How the return works (intended design): a cargo is funded by many participants together; the seller is paid at once, and proceeds return to token holders when the cargo settles. The same method is intended to extend to other real-world assets.

Built on an asset that is already liquid

Tokenising an asset makes it easier to divide and move, but it cannot create a market — tokens are only valuable if there are real buyers for the asset beneath them. Energy is one of the deepest, most actively traded markets in the world. The buyers and sellers already exist. MOOZARS tokenises assets that are already liquid — it connects to demand rather than inventing it.

How It Works

Stablecoin Settlement & Tokenised Real-World Assets

Stablecoin Settlement
A stablecoin is a digital currency backed 1:1 by fiat currency or approved reserve assets. It holds stable value — unlike volatile cryptocurrencies. MOOZARS is building infrastructure designed to settle commodity trades in seconds rather than the 2–5 days required by traditional correspondent banking. The intended design includes full reserve transparency and blockchain-verified settlement records, with counterparty risk reduced at the point of settlement.
Tokenized Capital Flow
Tokenization converts ownership of a real-world asset into digital tokens on a blockchain — enabling fractional ownership and instant transfer. MOOZARS is building infrastructure designed to tokenize commodity value at every stage of the supply chain: purchase, transport, refining, and storage. The intended model creates new capital pathways into markets that were previously accessible only to established trading houses.

Platform Services — Designed Capabilities

Issuance & Redemption
Designed around fiat-backed stablecoin issuance and redemption, with 1:1 reserve backing and full transparency as intended features. MOOZARS is designing its infrastructure with reference to HKMA, GENIUS Act (US), MiCA (EU), and FSMA frameworks, which it intends to operate under subject to obtaining the necessary licences. No licence is currently held and no application has yet been submitted.
Cross-Border Settlement
Designed to enable settlement across jurisdictions without correspondent banking delays or currency conversion risk. Blockchain-verified settlement records are an intended feature of the architecture. This is a design goal; the platform is not yet operational.
Reserve Transparency
The platform is designed with the intention that all stablecoin reserves will be held in approved instruments — fiat currency and treasury assets — and that reserve positions will be auditable and verifiable. These are design intentions for a platform in development.
Digital Asset Custody
Custody architecture is being designed to institutional standards, with hardware security and multi-signature authorisation as intended features for stablecoins, digital tokens, and tokenized commodity assets. No custody service is currently operational.
Compliance & KYC
The platform is being designed to include identity verification, sanctions screening, and AML compliance procedures, with reference to regulated institutional operations. These are design intentions; compliance infrastructure is in development.
